Artists

A sort name files an artist under another form of the name, e.g. "Alanis Morissette" as "Morissette, Alanis". It is used by the layout fields {albumartistsort} and {artistsort}. Suggestions come from your folders: a folder "Morissette, Alanis" suggests that sort name for "Alanis Morissette". "Keep as is" saves the name itself as the sort name, so none is suggested for it.
