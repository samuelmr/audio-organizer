Ratings

MusicMatch Jukebox kept your opinion of tracks as a "preference" (Excellent, Very Good, …) that only it and this tool read. Choose how many stars each preference is worth, and the files get star ratings that other players read. A rating a file already has is kept. The ratings go into the catalog first; you then write them into the files, as changesets you review.
