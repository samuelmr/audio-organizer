Album folders

{{ found.merges | length }} pairs of album folders share identical tracks. Merging one into the other trashes its copies of tracks the other already has (the other folder's copy is kept and playlists switch to it) and moves its remaining tracks over. A track whose number is already taken in the other folder is a clash – often a different recording or edition – and stays where it is. When no track stays, the folder's cover, booklet and other files move over too, so the folder disappears.

{{ found.batchable | length }} pairs have the same album name and no clashes and can be merged in batches; {{ others | length }} need a look; {{ found.pending }} have changes pending or waiting for review.
