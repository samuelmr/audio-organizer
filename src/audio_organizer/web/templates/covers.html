Covers

Phones, car stereos and many players only show covers embedded in the files. These tracks have no embedded cover, or only a thumbnail smaller than {{ min_pixels }} pixels, and their album has a cover: the image in its folder, or one that aorg coverart (or aorg scan --with-covers) found online. Embedding copies the image into every such track, so a large image makes every file bigger. A cover found online can also be saved as the folder's cover.jpg, for players that read folder images and for sync, when the folder holds just that album. Undoing the changeset removes the embedded covers and saved images again and puts back the thumbnails they replaced.

If the catalog was made before cover detection, run aorg scan --reread once; until then, files that already have a cover are left as they are when the changes are applied.
