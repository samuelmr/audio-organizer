Covers

Phones, car stereos and many players only show covers embedded in the files. These tracks have no embedded cover, or only a thumbnail smaller than {{ min_pixels }} pixels, and their album has a cover: the image in its folder, or one that aorg scan --deep found online. Embedding copies the image into every such track, so a large image makes every file bigger; undoing the changeset removes the embedded covers again and puts back the thumbnails they replaced.

If the catalog was made before cover detection, run aorg scan --reread once; until then, files that already have a cover are left as they are when the changes are applied.
